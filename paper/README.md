# The thesis

LaTeX template for a diploma thesis at WNE UW, in the traditional form or the form of a scientific
article. The layout follows the faculty's formal requirements (`wne.sty`); the rules are summarised in
`docs/wne-thesis-rules.md` of the seminar repository. The official documents take precedence.

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `metadata.tex` | Title, author, supervisor, abstract, keywords. Start here. |
| `thesis.tex` | The thesis: front matter, chapters or article + supplement, bibliography, lists. |
| `wne.sty` | Layout: fonts, margins, spacing, headings, captions, title and abstract pages. |
| `chapters/` | Chapters of the traditional form; literature, methodology and results double as the supplement in the article form. |
| `article/article.tex` | The article. |
| `references.bib` | Bibliography (export from Zotero with Better BibTeX). |
| `declarations/` | Put the scan of the signed faculty forms here as `declarations.pdf`. |
| `figures/` | Figures generated by code. |

## Build

```sh
make            # thesis.pdf
make article    # article/article.pdf, stand-alone
make clean
```

On Overleaf: compile `article/article.tex` with the extra line `\def\thesisbuild{}` at the top, save
the PDF as `article/article-thesis.pdf`, then compile `thesis.tex`.

## Choices

- **Article form:** uncomment `\articleformtrue` in `thesis.tex`.
- **Thesis in Polish:** set `\usepackage[polish]{babel}` in `wne.sty`, translate the fixed labels
  (title page, abstract page) and put the English title in `\thesistitleother`.
- **Two authors:** each signs their own declaration and states their contribution per chapter.

## Formal checklist

- Abstract at most 800 characters; at most 10 keywords (and their Polish translation for theses in English).
- Every table and figure: number, caption, `\source{...}` line, referenced in the text.
- Citations author--year, bibliography alphabetical.
- Signed declarations, including the declaration on the use of AI systems, in `declarations/declarations.pdf`.
